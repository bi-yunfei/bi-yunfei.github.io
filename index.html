Learning Trustworthy Human-Robot Collaboration Policies from Human-Human Demonstrations

通过人类-人类操作视频学习可信的人机交互扩散策略

Yunfei Bi毕云飞

Master's thesis, TUM, 2026 · paper to be submitted to IEEE RA-L硕士论文 · 慕尼黑工业大学 · 2026 年，论文拟投稿 IEEE RA-L

Collecting demonstrations of human-robot collaboration needs a robot, an operator and a partner at the same time. This thesis learns collaboration from markerless RGB videos of two people instead, plus a few teleoperated robot demonstrations, and makes the learned policy safe to run next to people.

  • HRC from human-human videos: the hand skeletons of both people are recovered with SAM 3 and HaMeR and triangulated in 3D; the acting hand is retargeted to the gripper and the partner's hand conditions a diffusion policy. Reading every video once per hand (role exchange) teaches both roles of a task, including roles with no robot demonstration (zero-shot).
  • Deployment-time embodiment-feasibility safety layer: the number of forward-diffusion noise steps needed to explain an action chunk as a noised feasible chunk serves as a feasibility score, learned from feasible chunks alone; a conformally calibrated threshold stops the robot before it attempts a motion its gripper cannot perform.
  • Results on a UR10e: 18/20 and 17/20 successful runs with five robot demonstrations per task (5/20 and 4/20 for a robot-only policy), 13/20 and 14/20 in zero-shot roles, and 19/20 and 18/20 infeasible motions stopped before contact.

人机协作示范的采集需要机器人、遥操作员和协作者同时在场，成本高、风险大。本课题改为从无标记的双人 RGB 视频中学习人机协作，辅以少量机器人遥操作示范，并保证学到的策略能在人身边安全部署。

  • 从双人视频学习人机协作：用 SAM 3 与 HaMeR 提取两人双手骨架并三角化到三维；执行手重定向为夹爪动作，搭档的手作为扩散策略的条件；通过角色互换让每段视频同时覆盖任务的两种角色，实现无机器人示范角色的零样本（zero-shot）学习。
  • 部署期本体可行性安全层：以“把动作块解释为加噪后的可行动作所需的扩散前向加噪步数”作为可行性评分，仅用可行动作训练；经保形（conformal）校准的阈值会在夹爪无法完成的动作接触物体之前叫停机器人。
  • UR10e 真机结果：每个任务仅用 5 条机器人示范，成功率达 18/20 和 17/20（纯机器人示范基线为 5/20 和 4/20）；零样本角色成功率 13/20 和 14/20；不可行动作在接触前被拦截 19/20 和 18/20 次。

CommonRoad-Game: A Human-in-the-Loop Simulation Framework for Autonomous Driving

一种人类在环的自动驾驶仿真框架

Yunfei Bi毕云飞, Youran Wang

arXiv preprint, 2026 · semester thesis at TUMarXiv 预印本 · 2026 年 · 第一作者 · 慕尼黑工业大学学期论文

Motion planners should be tested with humans in the loop, yet existing simulators often replay recorded datasets, lack interfaces for real-time human interaction, are only loosely tied to an autonomous-driving ecosystem, or are too heavy for rapid prototyping. CommonRoad-Game is a lightweight human-in-the-loop simulation framework tightly integrated with CommonRoad, for testing motion planners with human participants and for analyzing human driving in interactive scenarios.

  • Time synchronization: a multi-threaded architecture aligns simulation time with wall-clock time, so autonomous and human-driven vehicles interact deterministically and consistently in time; the drift drops from 402 ms to 1.5 ms.
  • Scenario generation: driving logs recorded during human-in-the-loop experiments become diverse, reproducible test cases.
  • Results: stable temporal synchronization, scalable multi-agent simulation, and seamless integration of CommonRoad-compatible motion planners such as IDM and a sampling-based reactive planner.

运动规划算法需要在人在环环境中测试，才能确保交互时行为安全高效；而现有仿真平台大多依赖录制数据集回放，缺少实时人机交互接口，与自动驾驶生态集成度低，或计算开销过大，不适合早期研究的快速原型验证。CommonRoad-Game 是与 CommonRoad 平台深度集成的轻量级人在环仿真框架，用于在真人参与下系统测试运动规划器，并分析交互场景中的人类驾驶行为。

  • 时间同步：多线程架构配合同步机制，使仿真时间与物理墙钟对齐，智驾车辆与人驾车辆的交互确定且时序一致，偏差从 402 ms 降至 1.5 ms。
  • 场景生成：记录人在环实验中的驾驶日志，构建多样且可复现的测试用例。
  • 实验结果：时间同步稳定，支持可扩展的多智能体仿真，并能无缝接入 IDM、基于采样的反应式规划器等兼容 CommonRoad 的运动规划器。
