M.Sc. student · Technical University of Munich慕尼黑工业大学 · 硕士在读

I am a master's student in Mechatronics, Robotics and Biomechanical Engineering at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). My research is on robot learning, in particular on learning from cross-embodiment data such as human videos and on human-robot interaction, with diffusion policies and vision-language-action (VLA) models. I have worked through the full loop, from deriving an algorithm and validating it in simulation to deploying it on real robots.

In my master's thesis at the Learning Systems and Robotics Lab of Prof. Angela P. Schoellig, I made two contributions. The first is a framework that learns human-robot collaboration policies from markerless RGB videos of two people, so that the robot also performs roles that no robot ever demonstrated (zero-shot). The second is a deployment-time safety layer that checks the embodiment feasibility of every action the policy emits and stops the robot before it attempts a motion its gripper cannot perform. Before that, I wrote my semester thesis at the Cyber-Physical Systems Group of Prof. Matthias Althoff, where I built CommonRoad-Game, a human-in-the-loop driving simulator released as a first-author arXiv preprint. I am currently a working student in the foundation-models team of Agile Robots SE, working on post-training and inference acceleration of VLA models.

Research研究成果

Learning Trustworthy Human-Robot Collaboration Policies from Human-Human Demonstrations

通过人类-人类操作视频学习可信的人机交互扩散策略

Yunfei Bi毕云飞

Master's thesis, TUM, 2026 · paper to be submitted to IEEE RA-L硕士论文 · 慕尼黑工业大学 · 2026 年，论文拟投稿 IEEE RA-L

A UR10e robot learns handovers and jar opening from markerless RGB videos of two people, including roles with no robot demonstration at all (zero-shot). An embodiment-feasibility safety layer, which measures feasibility in diffusion noise steps, stops motions the gripper cannot perform: 18/20 successful runs against 5/20 for a robot-only policy, and 19/20 infeasible motions stopped before contact.

基于 ROS 2 搭建 UR10e 遥操作系统，打通采集-训练-部署全流程；通过三维重建提取人手骨骼轨迹，与少量遥操作数据映射到统一动作空间协同训练扩散策略，并通过角色互换实现无机器人示范角色的零样本（zero-shot）学习；设计任务无关的动作块可行性度量，构建部署期安全层，无需人工标注即可对输出动作做可行性打分。真机实验中任务成功率 18/20（纯机器人示范基线为 5/20），不可行动作在接触前被拦截 19/20 次。

CommonRoad-Game: A Human-in-the-Loop Simulation Framework for Autonomous Driving

一种人类在环的自动驾驶仿真框架

Yunfei Bi毕云飞, Youran Wang

arXiv preprint, 2026 · semester thesis at TUMarXiv 预印本 · 2026 年 · 第一作者 · 慕尼黑工业大学学期论文

A lightweight human-in-the-loop driving simulator built into the CommonRoad platform, for testing motion planners against human drivers. Its multi-threaded real-time architecture cuts the drift between simulation time and wall-clock time from 402 ms to 1.5 ms. IDM and sampling-based planners are integrated, and recorded drives become reproducible test scenarios.

基于 CommonRoad 平台，主导设计并实现基于 Python 的人在环自动驾驶仿真系统：构建多线程实时仿真架构，解决仿真时间与物理墙钟的有界同步问题（偏差从 402 ms 降至 1.5 ms）；设计人驾与智驾车辆的同步机制，保证多模块在统一时间基准下稳定运行；集成并对比评估 IDM 与 Sampling-based Reactive Planner 等规划算法，支持可复现的复杂交通场景生成，为规控算法验证提供稳定的测试基建。
